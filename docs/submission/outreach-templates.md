# Outreach Templates (founder-sent; Prospector agent personalizes daily drafts)

## Cold email — v1 "missed call" (salons/barbers)

**Subject:** Missed calls at {{business}}?

Hi {{firstName|there}},

I called a few {{category}}s in {{city}} this week and most rang out — which
makes sense, you're with clients. Problem is, most callers who hit voicemail
just book with whoever answers next.

I built RingBack: when you miss a call, the caller instantly gets a text from
your number, an AI assistant answers their questions from YOUR service list,
and books them straight into your calendar. You get a summary text. Setup is
10 minutes (one call-forwarding code).

Founding customers get the first month for $1 — I'm looking for a handful of
{{city}} businesses to prove it out. Want a 2-minute demo? I'll call your
line, hang up, and you'll watch it book me in.

— Sebastien
built on Google Gemini · reply STOP if you'd rather not hear from me

## Walk-in pitch (30 seconds, live demo)

"Can I show you something on your own phone? Call this number and don't pick
up." [they call the demo line, hang up, get the textback, book] "That's what
your missed callers would get. First month's a dollar."

## Follow-up (3 days later)

**Subject:** re: missed calls — one number

{{firstName}}, one stat and I'll leave you alone: businesses like yours miss
roughly 4 in 10 calls, and ~85% of missed callers don't leave a voicemail —
they call the next place. RingBack texts them back in five seconds. First
month $1, cancel anytime, setup is one forwarding code. Worth 10 minutes?

## Testimonial ask (day-7 — sent by Onboarding agent, founder-approved copy)

Quick check-in from RingBack: how are the texted-back calls going? If it's
been useful, would you reply with one or two sentences we can quote? Real
words from real owners help us more than anything. If anything's off, reply
and a human jumps in.
